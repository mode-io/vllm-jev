- The experimental Windows [Eval Engine Decision](https://huggingface.co/evalengine/decision-0.8b-gguf) adapters follow the published JSON prompt and complete candidate-letter logit protocol for the fixed 0.8B Q8_0 and 4B Q4_K_M releases. Their Apache-2.0 LICENSE/NOTICE files remain in the verified downloads. The native runtime is the fixed official MIT-licensed llama-cpp-python 0.3.35 CPU wheel, with its packaged notices and the retained [llama.cpp notice](licenses/llama.cpp-MIT.txt). The independent validation reference implements the published protocol separately; it is not an unchanged original-author SDK. Same-artifact validation does not establish BF16 equivalence. See [Windows implementation and acceptance](docs/windows-next-four.md).
- The Windows [Bruv1-0.8B](https://huggingface.co/bvolpato/bruv1-0.8b) adapter follows the prompt and restricted label-head scorer in [bvolpato/bruv](https://github.com/bvolpato/bruv/tree/d10751e8fd3c2a917fea81fb350593eeed3c482d). The author's MIT notice is retained in [licenses/Bruv-MIT.txt](licenses/Bruv-MIT.txt); the separately downloaded weights retain their Apache-2.0 license. The reference executes an unchanged, hash-verified minimal copy of the original encoder/collator/scorer in a private process. - The Windows RSI v1.0-0.8B adapter follows the [fixed HF release](https://huggingface.co/shgao/rsi-jev-v1.0-qwen3.5-0.8b/tree/f9248caceb89caf2e6c968ea33bf0d6eb7f957b0) and reconstructs its text tower and option cross-attention scorer using installed libraries. - The experimental Windows Decider adapter follows the published Decider answer-slot prompt, BF16 restricted-vocabulary projection and isolated Score-level calculation.
