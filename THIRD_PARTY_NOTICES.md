# Third-party notices

- [Tev](https://github.com/togethercomputer/tev1) publishes its prompt recipe under MIT. The task-JSON adapter follows that recipe; its notice is preserved in [licenses/Tev-MIT.txt](licenses/Tev-MIT.txt). Model weight licenses are separate from the recipe license.

- [JevK5](https://github.com/allebee/jevk5) is licensed under Apache-2.0. The decision adapter follows its prompt, answer-letter readout, and checkpoint calibration. Its prompt derives from [SemIf](https://github.com/TheoLeeCJ/SemIf-OpenJev), MIT License; the notice is preserved in [licenses/SemIf-MIT.txt](licenses/SemIf-MIT.txt). Weights are downloaded separately; upstream inference code is not vendored.
- [Kev](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev), [Decider](https://github.com/Mapika/decider), and [Mica](https://github.com/akivet/Mica-v0.1-4B) publish their inference protocols under Apache-2.0. The decision adapters follow their prompt formats, readout positions, and calibration settings. Model weights are downloaded separately; upstream runtimes are not vendored. These notices do not imply that every upstream checkpoint has been validated.
- [This-That](https://github.com/FLock-io/this-that-model) is licensed under MIT. The decision adapter follows its packed question prompts and label readouts. Its notice is preserved in [licenses/This-That-MIT.txt](licenses/This-That-MIT.txt). Weights are downloaded separately; upstream runtimes are not vendored.
- [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm) is licensed under Apache-2.0. This project integrates through vLLM's pooling and plugin interfaces and uses its Qwen3.5 model class and hybrid-cache hooks at runtime. vLLM source and binary artifacts are not vendored here.
- [Open-Jev](https://github.com/Zefan-Cai/Open-Jev) code is licensed under MIT. `vllm_jev.prompt` follows its candidate prompt format, the optional `/v1/systemone` route follows its typed decision schema, and `vllm_jev.export` consumes its LoRA/scalar-head packages. Its license notice is preserved in [licenses/Open-Jev-MIT.txt](licenses/Open-Jev-MIT.txt). The public [2B](https://huggingface.co/ZefanCai/Open-Jev-2B) and [9B](https://huggingface.co/ZefanCai/Open-Jev-9B) adapter/head weights are Apache-2.0 and are downloaded separately; they are not vendored here.
- [OpenJev-0.6B](https://huggingface.co/IamBusy/OpenJev-0.6B) is released under Apache-2.0. The `openjev_branch_v03` adapter follows its public candidate-branch and calibration format. Its Qwen3 base and adapter/head weights are downloaded separately.
- [Laya](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya) is released under Apache-2.0. Linux uses its pinned prompt and trained decision head with vLLM's ModernBERT encoder; Mac uses its published PyTorch MPS runtime. The [English](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya), [multilingual](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya-multilingual), and [typed-decision](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya-typed-decisions) weights are downloaded separately; no weights or source code are vendored.
- [Tiny-Jev](https://huggingface.co/lostargon/Tiny-Jev) is released under Apache-2.0. The `tiny_jev_marker` adapter follows its published option-marker positions and decision-head calculation. Its weights are downloaded separately; its custom Python model file is not executed or vendored.
- [Valen](https://github.com/Liuziyu77/Valen) is released under Apache-2.0. The `valen_qwen_v1` adapter follows its published candidate compiler and projection readout for inference. Its [preview weights](https://huggingface.co/Valen-Team/Valen-Preview-0923) are downloaded separately and are not vendored.
- [RSI-Jev](https://github.com/Shanghua-Gao/RSI-Jev) code is licensed under MIT. The `rsijev_xattn_v1` adapter follows its published prompt encoding, option cross-attention readout, and calibration; its notice is preserved in [licenses/RSI-Jev-MIT.txt](licenses/RSI-Jev-MIT.txt). The [v3.0](https://huggingface.co/shgao/rsi-jev-v3.0-qwen3.5-2b) and [v4.0-VL](https://huggingface.co/shgao/rsi-jev-v4.0-vl-qwen3.5-2b) weights are Apache-2.0 and are downloaded separately; no weights are vendored.

- [Clef](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef) and [Clef-Flash](https://huggingface.co/Cloudflare/clef-flash) are released by Cloudflare under Apache-2.0. The `clef_joint_v1` adapter follows their published `joint_schema_model.py`: the prompt format, the joint schema head, and the token-position readout. The release code is not executed or vendored here; only the selected checkpoint's released weight shards and head file are linked during export. Model weights are downloaded separately.
- [vjev-vision](https://huggingface.co/yah01/vjev-vision) and its [pilot](https://huggingface.co/yah01/vjev-vision-pilot) are released under Apache-2.0. The `vjev_vision_v1` adapter follows their published trailing option readout over Qwen3.5 vision tokens. Model weights are downloaded separately and are not vendored; the author's [vjev-serve](https://github.com/BubbleCal/vjev-serve) code is not included.
- The README's Sokoban video uses game frames from [Valen-Eval-Game](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Valen-Team/Valen-Eval-Game), captured during live inference. The original dataset is not included in this repository.
- The README's Mac demo uses Maze screenshots from [GameQA-140K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenMOSS-Team/GameQA-140K), via [Valen-Eval-General-5k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Valen-Team/Valen-Eval-General-5k). The upstream dataset card declares MIT; see [licenses/GameQA-MIT.txt](licenses/GameQA-MIT.txt). Only demo media is included, not the dataset.
- [MLX](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx) and [MLX-VLM](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-vlm) provide Apple Silicon inference at runtime. Their source and model weights are not vendored here.
- [Qwen3](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B) and [Qwen3.5](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B) base weights are downloaded separately and are not included in this repository.
