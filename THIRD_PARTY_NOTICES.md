- [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm) is licensed under Apache-2.0. This project integrates through vLLM's pooling and plugin interfaces and uses its Qwen3.5 model class and hybrid-cache hooks at runtime. vLLM source and binary artifacts are not vendored here.
- [Open-Jev](https://github.com/Zefan-Cai/Open-Jev) code is licensed under MIT. `vllm_jev.prompt` follows its candidate prompt format, the optional `/v1/systemone` route follows its typed decision schema, and `vllm_jev.export` consumes its LoRA/scalar-head packages. Its license notice is preserved in [licenses/Open-Jev-MIT.txt](licenses/Open-Jev-MIT.txt). The public [2B](https://huggingface.co/ZefanCai/Open-Jev-2B) and [9B](https://huggingface.co/ZefanCai/Open-Jev-9B) adapter/head weights are Apache-2.0 and are downloaded separately; they are not vendored here.
- [OpenJev-0.6B](https://huggingface.co/IamBusy/OpenJev-0.6B) is released under Apache-2.0. The `openjev_branch_v03` adapter follows its public candidate-branch and calibration format. Its Qwen3 base and adapter/head weights are downloaded separately.
- [Tiny-Jev](https://huggingface.co/lostargon/Tiny-Jev) is released under Apache-2.0. The `tiny_jev_marker` adapter follows its published option-marker positions and decision-head calculation. Its weights are downloaded separately; its custom Python model file is not executed or vendored.
- [Valen](https://github.com/Liuziyu77/Valen) is released under Apache-2.0. The `valen_qwen_v1` adapter follows its published candidate compiler and projection readout for inference. Its [preview weights](https://huggingface.co/Valen-Team/Valen-Preview-0923) are downloaded separately and are not vendored.
- [vjev-vision](https://huggingface.co/yah01/vjev-vision) and its [pilot](https://huggingface.co/yah01/vjev-vision-pilot) are released under Apache-2.0. The `vjev_vision_v1` adapter follows their published trailing option readout over Qwen3.5 vision tokens. Model weights are downloaded separately and are not vendored; the author's [vjev-serve](https://github.com/BubbleCal/vjev-serve) code is not included.
- The README's Sokoban video uses game frames from [Valen-Eval-Game](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Valen-Team/Valen-Eval-Game), captured during live inference. The original dataset is not included in this repository.
- [Qwen3](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B) and [Qwen3.5](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B) base weights are downloaded separately and are not included in this repository.
